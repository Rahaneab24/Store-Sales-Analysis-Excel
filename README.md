# 👨🏻‍💻 Store Sales Analysis—Excel
Annual retail sales analysis (2022) built entirely in Excel using PivotTables, covering ~31,000 orders across multiple e-commerce channels, states, and customer demographics.

## 📊 Project Overview

This project analyzes a full year of store order data (2022) to uncover customer, channel, and regional sales trends, and translates the findings into actionable business recommendations — a complete Excel-based analyst workflow from raw data to insight.

## 🗂️ Dataset
~31,000 orders, with fields including Order ID, Customer ID, Gender, Age, Date, Order Status, Sales Channel, Product Category, Size, Quantity, Amount, and Shipping Location<br>
Single source file with a Data sheet feeding all PivotTable analysis

## 🛠️ Tools Used
Microsoft Excel — PivotTables, PivotCharts, data aggregation


## 📈 Analysis Performed
Age vs. Gender breakdown — order share split by age group (Teenager/Adult/Senior) and gender<br>
Sales channel performance — order distribution across Amazon, Flipkart, Myntra, Ajio, Meesho, Nalli, and others<br>
Order status tracking — Delivered, Cancelled, Returned, and Refunded order counts<br>
Revenue by state — top-performing states by total sales amount<br>
Revenue by gender — total sales contribution split between men and women<br>
Monthly sales trend — order count and revenue by month across the full year<br>

## 💡 Key Insights
Women account for ~65% of purchases, generating ₹13.56M in sales vs. ₹7.61M from men<br>
Maharashtra, Karnataka, and Uttar Pradesh are the top 3 states, contributing ~35% of total orders<br>
The Adult (30–40 yrs) age group is the largest contributor, at ~50% of orders<br>
Amazon, Flipkart, and Myntra together account for ~80% of all orders<br>
Recommended targeting women aged 30–40 in the top 3 states via ads/offers on the top 3 channels<br>
